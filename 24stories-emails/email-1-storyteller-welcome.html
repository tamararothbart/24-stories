How it works

Each prompt leads to one chapter.

Each chapter is intended as a self-contained story.

Each story can be as long or as short as you like — a family joke or an epic chronicle of your career.

The stories do not need to be told in chronological order from childhood to later years. Tell them as the memories surface.

You can ignore the prompts and tell whatever story you wish to record on the day. 24 Stories is designed to simply give you the space, place, and time to start telling your loved ones who you are.

The finished product, your collected stories, will be more than the sum of its parts.
