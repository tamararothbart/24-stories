Hello [StorytellerFirstName],

You are nearly there. Two more stories to tell. While you tell them, we want to make sure your Collected Stories are ready for print the moment you press Complete.

There are four things to do in your Story Library. Some you may have already done — if so, you're ahead of the game!

  1. 1. Tell any skipped stories Your library holds all 26 prompts. If there are weeks you have not recorded, use your library to do so now. You are not obligated to respond to all 26 prompts. If you choose to skip some, your book will simply have fewer chapters.
  2. 2. Add missing photographs and captions Each chapter has room for one captioned image — colour or black and white. If you have not yet uploaded them, do so before you press Complete.
  3. 3. Add your book details Title The title field has a default suggestion: Dear Family — the Collected Stories of [Your Full Name]. Keep it by adding your name in the placeholder, or choose your own title — as long as it does not exceed 30 characters. Portrait photograph This appears on the first page of your book. Caption it with your name (and your maiden name if applicable), date and place of birth. Dedication and epigraph The dedication and epigraph are optional extras.
  4. 4. Delivery details and number of copies Fill in your physical address for delivery. Including a mobile number is optional. It's so that the courier can alert you when your book is on its way. Confirm the number of copies you would like to order.
Press Complete

When all your stories are told and your book form is filled in, press the Mark as Complete button in your library.

After you press Complete, no further changes or additions are possible. Your Collected Stories go straight into production.

Allow up to four weeks from print to delivery. We take great care to make your book beautiful.
