How to save each template to Gmail:
1. Click the gold Select this email button — the whole email body highlights automatically.
2. Cmd+C to copy.
3. Open Gmail → Compose → Cmd+V to paste.
4. Click ⋮ (three dots, bottom right of compose) → Templates → Save draft as template → Save as new template. Name it and done.
5. Fill in placeholders when sending — they match the surrounding text so the email looks seamless.
