## The Product

**24 Stories — The Complete Legacy Journey**

A six-month guided storytelling experience that ends with a professionally printed hardcover book of their life stories. Given as a gift or purchased for yourself.

---

## What Is Included (R6,795)

- 26 weekly story prompts — curated, with alternative angles (not full alternatives — just alternative words/framings per prompt)
- Professional editing on every story (Tamara reads and polishes every submission — no competitor does this)
- Each story shared with the whole family by email as it is written — not held until the end
- Personal Story Library — private portal where stories are saved and photos uploaded
- One beautifully designed, printed hardcover Legacy Book
- Delivered to your door

**Extra copies: R1,200 each** — ordered at proof stage, added to same print run.

## The Wording Decisions

**Hero copy (approved):** "The stories only they can tell."
- Works for gift buyer ("they" = their parent/grandparent)
- Works for self-buyer ("they" = I, the only one who can tell my own stories)
- Do not change without discussion

**Gift vs self-purchase:** Straddle both. Never pick a side.
- Use receiver/storyteller-focused language throughout
- "Their stories", "your loved one", "the storyteller" — all work for both audiences
- Signup form has two explicit paths (giving as gift / telling my own story) — this is where the divergence happens, not in the hero copy
- StoryWorth, Remento, Circa Legacy all straddle. Tell Mel picked gift only — least effective.

**"Book" not "memoir"** — book language throughout all copy and emails. Memoir removed.

## Key Research Findings (locked, do not re-research)

- **Remento book cost (Shark Tank disclosure):** $16 to print and ship — achievable only at volume with Blurb API bulk rates. Not achievable at <50 books/year.
- **Blurb API:** Accessible, no minimums, but requires finished PDF. Does NOT automate layout. At low volume costs R1,775–2,495/book including SA shipping. Not viable.
- **Local SA printer:** ~R1,100/book + R150 Courier Guy = R1,250. Correct model at current scale.
- **StoryWorth book automation:** Under research — how do they produce fully automated books at such low cost? If their automation approach is replicable, may change book production decision.
- **Book template:** A designer will fill in the template and send to print. Canva vs Reedsy decision deferred until automation research is complete. Do not lock either until StoryWorth automation is understood.
