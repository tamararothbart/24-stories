## OpenClaw — What It Is

OpenClaw is a free, open-source, self-hosted autonomous AI agent that runs on Tamara's local Mac. It manages emails, calendars, and files, and connects with WhatsApp, Telegram, and Discord. It executes commands rather than just generating text. It can send outreach emails, manage follow-up sequences, send WhatsApp messages, and schedule calendar appointments — autonomously.

**Setup required (Tamara, one-time, ~2 hours):**
- Install from OpenClaw GitHub on Mac
- Connect: stories@24stories.co.za email, WhatsApp Business account, calendar
- Configure daily outreach limits, working hours, tone
- Then hand it the OpenClaw task brief that Claude Code produces

## Family Recipient Re-engagement Campaign
A Mailjet broadcast email sent manually to family recipients at strategic points during the 6-month story journey (e.g. Week 6 and Week 12). Three purposes in one email:
1. Encourage recipients to give the storyteller direct feedback — creates emotional momentum
2. Suggest stories they'd like to hear — organic engagement, no technology required, just conversation
3. Soft referral CTA: "Do you have stories worth telling? Someone in your family might want to give you the same gift."
- These recipients are the warmest possible leads — they've already experienced the product
- No technical build needed — manual Mailjet broadcast campaign
- Timing: Week 6 (early enough to convert) and Week 12 (midpoint re-engagement)
- Add to campaign session brief when building the full marketing plan
