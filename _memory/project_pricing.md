# 24 Stories — Final Pricing (v3.1 confirmed)

**Trigger phrase: "pricing"**
**Locked: 2026-04-10**

---

## The Offer

**R6,795 — once-off, upfront, everything included.**

- 26 weekly story prompts (with 2 alternatives each)
- Professional editing on every story (Tamara — key differentiator, competitors do zero editing)
- Stories shared with family each week
- Story Library with photograph upload per story
- One professionally designed, printed hardcover Legacy Book
- Delivered to your door (Courier Guy)

No tiers. No monthly billing. No upgrades. One payment, complete journey.

**Extra copies: R1,200 each** — ordered at proof stage, added to same print run.

---

## Why once-off upfront (not monthly subscription)

- Gift buyers need a defined cost and outcome — monthly billing creates commitment anxiety
- Simpler Make build — no failed payment loop, no cancellation flow complexity
- Cleaner emotional narrative: one payment, six-month journey, ends with a book
- StoryWorth and Remento both moved to annual/one-off for the same reasons

## Cost Build-Up Per Subscriber

| Item | At 5 subscribers | At 20 subscribers |
|------|-----------------|------------------|
| App costs (Airtable + Make + Mailjet) | R1,140 | R285 |
| Editing: 24 stories × 25 min × R250/hr | R2,500 | R2,500 |
| Print (hardcover, full colour, local SA printer) | R1,100 | R1,100 |
| Courier Guy delivery | R150 | R150 |
| PayFast once-off fee (~3.5%) | R203 | R203 |
| **Total cost** | **~R5,093** | **~R4,238** |
| **Revenue** | **R6,795** | **R6,795** |
| **Margin** | **R702 (12%)** | **R1,557 (27%)** |

Margins grow naturally as app costs spread. Do not raise base price to compensate for low early volume.

## Book Production — Local SA Printer (not Blurb API)

**Blurb API rejected for current scale:**
- Per-book cost: $46–65 (~R850–1,200) + shipping to SA $50–70 (~R925–1,295)
- Total via Blurb: R1,775–2,495 per book
- Does NOT automate layout — still requires a finished PDF
- Not worth pursuing until 50+ books/year

**Current production model:**
- PDF compiled by Tamara using Reedsy Book Editor (free) or Canva (free) — template built once, reused
- Sent to local SA printer: ~R1,100 per book (user-confirmed figure)
- Courier Guy delivery: ~R150 per book
- **Total book production cost: ~R1,250**
- Revisit automation/Blurb API when volume justifies it

## Escalation Path

1. **Launch:** R6,795 once-off
2. **Extra copies:** R1,200 each — immediate margin boost
3. **After 10–20 subscribers + proof of concept:** raise base price to R6,500 (no announcement — just change it)
4. **App costs:** fall sharply with volume — bank the margin, do not reprice downward
5. **Editing efficiency:** improves with practice — effective hourly rate improves over time

## Competitor Editing Benchmark

- **StoryWorth:** Zero editing. Basic spell-check only. Stories go in as typed.
- **Remento:** AI transcription only. No human editor. Stories go in as spoken.
- **24 Stories:** Professional human editing on every story. This is the premium differentiator and justifies the price premium over R1,850 (StoryWorth SA equivalent including shipping).
