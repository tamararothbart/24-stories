# 24 Stories — Prompt Schedule (26 Weeks)

Status: **LOCKED — finalised 2026-04-06. Ready for Airtable entry.**

---

Week 1 — Inheritance
Theme: "I got it from my mama/papa"
Prompt: Prepare a five-minute story about something you inherited — jokes, traditions, taste.
Other angles: Heirlooms, Traits, Family Lore

Week 2 — Youthful Imaginings
Theme: Beginnings
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about unfulfilled hopes, wild ambitions, or the thing you always dreamed you'd become (but never did).
Other angles: Compromise, Rite of Passage, Dawning Realizations

Week 3 — Relationships
Theme: Family Matters
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about the people you're bound to by blood, marriage, or choice… holiday meltdowns, group texts gone rogue, unconditional love, or long‑held grudges.
Other angles: Parents, Siblings, Cousins

Week 4 — Childhood
Theme: School Days
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about something or someone unforgettable from school — awkward, hilarious, or life‑defining.
Other angles: Growing Up, Playground, Bullies, Mentors

Week 5 — Faith
Theme: Leap of Faith
Prompt: Prepare a five-minute story about a big risk you took without knowing where you'd land.
Other angles: Religion, Miracle, Caution to the Wind

Week 6 — Heartbreak
Theme: Love Hurts
Prompt: Prepare a five-minute story of a love that made you go OUCH — the agony of deferred love, the misery of good love gone bad, or the anguish of unrequited love.
Other angles: The One that Got Away, Breaking Up, Blind Date

Week 7 — True Love
Theme: Falling
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about your earliest romantic awakening, your first love, or your forever love.
Other angles: Cupid's Target, Wedding Bells, Romantic Awakenings

Week 8 — Conflict
Theme: Dodgy
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about deception or suspicion, being played for a fool, or calling someone out.
Other angles: Enemies, Rivalry, Interference

Week 9 — Chaos
Theme: Disasterpiece
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a disaster so epic it became its own work of art — chaotic, messy, unforgettable.
Other angles: Oops!, Dropping the ball, Misread

Week 10 — Risk
Theme: Trouble
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about defying authority, a gamble that paid off — or didn't.
Other angles: Crime and Punishment, Breaking the Rules, Caught in the Act

Week 11 — Adventure & Travel
Theme: On the Road
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a journey, physical or emotional, that changed you.
Other angles: Lost, Far from Home, Detour

Week 12 — Transformation
Theme: Second Chance
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a fresh start, a new chapter, a turning point, or the moment you finally snapped and walked away — and what came of it.
Other angles: Starting Over, Reset, Reinvention

Week 13 — Work
Theme: On the Job
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about the work you did, the work you wanted, or the job that surprised you.
Other angles: Boss, Hustle, Reward

Week 14 — Setbacks
Theme: Failure
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a time when things didn't go your way, when you went too far, or discovered your limits.
Other angles: Reputation, Lesson Learned, Getting Up — Again

Week 15 — Fate
Theme: Luck
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a lucky break, a twist of fate, or the unexpected working in your favor.
Other angles: Right Place, Wrong Time; Pulling Sticks, Serendipity

Week 16 — Secrets
Theme: Shock, Scandal, and Raised Eyebrows
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a secret you kept — or a secret that came out.
Other angles: Skeletons in the Closet, Confession, Taboo

Week 17 — Parenting
Theme: Growing Pains
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about the time your child became your teacher.
Other angles: Motherhood/Fatherhood, What Could Go Wrong?, Plot Twist

Week 18 — Losing
Theme: Goodbye
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about loss — letting go of something or someone you thought you'd always have.
Other angles: Death, Acceptance, Forgiveness

Week 19 — The Unexpected
Theme: Strange Encounters
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about unexpected brushes with the peculiar, the wild, or the unforgettable.
Other angles: Truth is Stranger than Fiction, Out of the Ordinary, Crazy World

Week 20 — Courage
Theme: Guts
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a moment when you had to be brave.
Other angles: Reluctant Hero, Stronger than you think, The Right Thing

Week 21 — Gratitude
Theme: Blessings
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about someone or something you are truly grateful for.
Other angles: Joy, Kindness, Ticking off the Bucket List

Week 22 — Ambition
Theme: Sweat and Tears
Prompt: Prepare a five-minute story about keeping your eye on the prize; cut‑throat tactics or keeping your nose to the grindstone.
Other angles: Drive, Sacrifice, The Big Break

Week 23 — Narrow Escapes
Theme: Danger and Defiance
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about a narrow escape — a moment you were almost in trouble, but somehow came out unscathed.
Other angles: Life and Death, What If?, Miracles

Week 24 — If I Knew Then
Theme: Lessons for my Future
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about something you wish you could have told your younger self.
Other angles: Hindsight, Wide Angle Lens

Week 25 — Legacy (Part I)
Theme: Home and Heritage
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about the place you first called home and what it means now.
Other angles: Family Matters, Roots, Bloodline

Week 26 — Legacy (Part 2)
Theme: Wisdom (reprise Week 2)
Prompt: Prepare a five‑minute story about what you hope will endure in your life — dreams, lessons, loves, memories.
Other angles: What Remains
