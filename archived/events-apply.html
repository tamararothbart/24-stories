How This Works

Each 24 Stories Live evening is built around a single theme. We review every application with our current and upcoming themes in mind — and we will be in touch when we feel your story is the right fit, even where the connection is a stretch. This may mean your story is held for a future event rather than the next one.

Every selected storyteller takes part in a short 15-minute preparation conversation with a qualified 24 Stories team member before the event — a chance to shape the story for the room, find where to begin, and decide where to land.
