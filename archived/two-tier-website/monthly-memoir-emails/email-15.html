MM EMAIL 15 — NOT APPLICABLE (MONTHLY MEMOIR)
Bound Edition equivalent: email-15-pause-confirmation.html
Status: ⛔ NOT APPLICABLE — PAUSE IS BOUND EDITION ONLY

Email 15 does not apply to Monthly Memoir subscribers.

In the Bound Edition flow, email-15 confirms a subscription pause. Bound Edition subscribers (upfront payers) may pause for up to 12 months from their subscription start date.

Monthly Memoir subscribers cancel — they do not pause.

Monthly Memoir runs month-to-month. There is no need to pause — a subscriber can simply cancel and the debit stops. They may re-subscribe at the standard rate at a future date, but there is no guaranteed hold on their journey.

Cancellation is handled by Email D.
