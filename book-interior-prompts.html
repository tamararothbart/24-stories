Book Interior · ChatGPT Prompts · 19 April 2026
What this file is. Copy-paste ready prompts for ChatGPT (GPT-4o with image generation). Chapters 1–6 plus Chapter 26 (the last chapter). Each chapter has an image prompt and a caption prompt. Separate prompts cover the portrait, front matter pages, and page layout mockups.

Chapter title rule. The category name (Inheritance, Childhood Dreams, etc.) is used here as context for ChatGPT image generation only. It does not appear in the book. The chapter heading that appears in the book is the title below it — e.g. "I Got It From My Mama," "Books and Smoking Guns."

Chapters 7–25 are pending. When Tamara provides those titles, add them here following the same pattern as the chapters below.

Dedication and epigraph pages have no labels. No word "Dedication" or "Epigraph" appears anywhere in the book.
Character consistency — read before starting.
ChatGPT GPT-4o can hold a consistent face across images. Exact workflow:
  1. Step 1. Run Prompt 1 (portrait of Mary J. Miller). Save the image.
  2. Step 2. For every prompt where Mary should appear, upload the saved portrait into the same ChatGPT chat and add: "The woman in the uploaded photo is Mary. Use her face in this image."
  3. Step 3. If her face changes between images: "Regenerate — keep Mary's face exactly as in the reference photo."
  4. Mary's age per chapter: Ch 2 → age 7–8 · Ch 3 → age 10–12 (group) · Ch 4 → age 10–11 · Ch 5 → mid-thirties · Ch 6 → late thirties · Ch 26 → late sixties.
