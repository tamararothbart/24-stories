Typography — Cover Specimen
Title font Nexa Black by Fontfabric — identified via WhatTheFont (returned Nexa Rust Sans Black 2 as closest match; clean Black variant is correct — see note below).

Secondary visual candidate: Trade Gothic Bold Condensed No. 20 — also strong visual match.

Free alternative for HTML mockup and specimens: Bebas Neue — available free at fonts.google.com. Very close condensed black grotesque. Suitable for mockup use; not for final print file.
Font source Fontfabric — fontfabric.com
Nexa Black (single weight): from $35 USD
Complete Nexa family (5 fonts): $45 USD
Check fontfabric.com — Nexa Black sometimes offered free for personal use
⚠ Rust variant warning Do NOT use Nexa Rust Sans Black 2. The "Rust" suffix denotes a distressed/textured variant with rough scratched edges. The cover text is clean and smooth. The correct variant is Nexa Black or Nexa Heavy — no Rust, no texture.
Title weight Black / Extra Bold — clean, no distressing
Title case All caps
Title tracking Very slightly wide — letters not tightly packed. Approximately 5–10 units. Confirm from final artwork file
Title size Each line occupies approximately 35–40% of cover width. Exact point size depends on final artwork file. Confirm from final artwork file
"The Complete Stories of" Same font family, condensed. All caps, tracking noticeably wide. Approximately 40–45% of title size.
"Mary J. Miller" Same font family, Black weight. All caps, normal to slightly wide tracking. Approximately 55–60% of title size — slightly larger than the line above.
Spine text Same font family, lighter weight — regular or medium condensed. Small size, all caps, normal tracking.
Font identification method WhatTheFont (myfonts.com/pages/whatthefont) — screenshot of title cropped from Amazon product PDF, uploaded as PNG. Result: Nexa Rust Sans Black 2 by Fontfabric. Clean variant confirmed as correct by visual analysis.
