Who this is for

Some prompts stop you cold. A memory that's too big, a story that's too complicated, or simply the feeling that what you wrote doesn't capture what you meant. That's what coaching is for.

If 24 Stories was given to you as a gift, coaching is a way to honour that gift — to arrive at each story prepared, and to leave each session knowing you've told it as well as it can be told.

How a session works
  1. Submit your story or prompt idea 24 hours before your session Send Tamara what you have — a draft, a voice note, a paragraph, or just the prompt you're working on.
  2. Tamara reads it and prepares Before you meet, your story is read in full. Tamara arrives knowing exactly where you are.
  3. You spend one hour together — by video or phone A focused, personal conversation about your story, your voice, and what the story is really about.
  4. You leave knowing exactly what your story is about and how to tell it Not just notes. A clear path forward — so you can sit down and write with confidence.
