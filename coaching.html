Who This Is For

How It Works

  1. To get the most from each session, it helps to submit your story beforehand. That said, you are welcome to enter a session without a prepared story — it is entirely up to you.

  2. We suggest booking a single session before deciding whether to commit to a four-session bundle or the full 24-week programme.

  3. The hourly rate is R650. This includes a preparatory read of your story, provided it is submitted at least 24 hours before the session begins.

All sessions are conducted personally by Tamara. Availability is limited to ten new admissions per month. Early booking is recommended.
