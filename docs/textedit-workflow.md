# TextEdit Workflow

TextEdit is 24 Stories' automated story editing engine. It uses Claude AI to produce a structured edit of every raw story submission, generating both a chapter title and an edited version of the text.

---

## Story Fields (Airtable — Stories table)

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| StoryText | multilineText | Raw story as submitted by the storyteller. Never modified after submission. |
| EditedText | multilineText | TextEdit's AI-generated edit. Fully editable — Tamara can open any record and type, cut, paste, rewrite freely. |
| FinalStory | multilineText | Tamara's manually approved final version. Populated after comparing StoryText with EditedText. Fully editable. |
| ChapterTitle | singleLineText | Chapter title generated by TextEdit alongside EditedText. Editable. |

---

## Step-by-Step Flow

### 1. Storyteller submits a story
- Storyteller records voice or types text on tell.html
- Voice recordings are transcribed via OpenAI Whisper (`transcribe.js`)
- Text is optionally cleaned via Claude (`cleanup.js`) — light pass for readability before submission
- `story-submission.js` saves the story to the Stories table, writing it to **StoryText**
- Tamara receives an alert email at hello@24stories.co.za

### 2. TextEdit runs automatically
- `text-edit-queue.js` runs on a scheduled cron (every 5 minutes via Netlify)
- It queries Stories for records where `StoryText` is set but `ChapterTitle` is empty — these are unedited stories
- For each unedited story, it sends `StoryText` to Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6) with a detailed editorial system prompt
- Claude returns two outputs:
  - **TITLE:** — a chapter title derived from the story's content
  - **EDITED STORY:** — a properly edited version of the story
- TextEdit writes both back to Airtable: `ChapterTitle` and `EditedText`
- StoryText is never touched

### 3. Tamara reviews the edit
- Open the Airtable Stories record
- Compare **StoryText** (raw submission) with **EditedText** (TextEdit output)
- If the edit is good: copy EditedText into **FinalStory**, or retype directly in FinalStory
- If the edit needs work: retype or revise directly in FinalStory, using EditedText as a starting point
- FinalStory is what goes to the family and into the book

### 4. Story goes to family
- Tamara approves the story in the Story Library (library.html) or via the approve-story.js flow
- `SendToFamily` checkbox is ticked (in Airtable or via library.html)
- `send-story-queue.js` detects the tick and sends the story to the subscriber's family list

---

## Field Editability

Both `EditedText` and `FinalStory` are `multilineText` fields in Airtable. This means:
- Tamara can open any record in Airtable and type, cut, paste, and rewrite directly
- No formula or locked formatting — plain text only
- No hidden formatting that breaks on paste

---

## Note on FinalStory

FinalStory is a trial field. Once TextEdit has been tested across enough stories and Tamara is confident the AI edits meet the required standard, FinalStory may be removed and EditedText used directly as the approved version. Until then, FinalStory is the deliberate separation point between machine output and human approval.
