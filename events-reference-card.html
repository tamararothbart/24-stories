Your email hello@24stories.co.za — all replies go from here

Someone asks a general question about 24 Stories Live

  1. Reply from hello@24stories.co.za with event details (theme, date, venue when confirmed)
  2. Direct them to 24stories.co.za/events.html to join the mailing list if not already signed up
  3. Update their Status in Airtable → Community table → Contacted

Someone signs up via Stay in the Loop (event interest)

  1. Netlify sends notification to hello@24stories.co.za
  2. Paste the email to Claude — Claude creates the Airtable record (Community table)
  3. When event date is confirmed — email them with theme, date, venue and ticket details
  4. Update Status → Attending once they confirm

Someone ticks "I'm interested in telling a story"

  1. Netlify sends notification to hello@24stories.co.za
  2. Paste the email to Claude — Claude creates the Airtable record (Community table, Status: New)
  3. Reply from hello@ — introduce yourself, send the link: 24stories.co.za/events-apply.html
  4. Update their Status → Form Sent
  5. When they submit the application form, Netlify sends another notification to hello@
  6. Paste that email to Claude — Claude creates the Airtable record (Storyteller Applications table)
  7. Update Community table Status → Application Received
  8. Review their application — reply with next steps (prep call details if selected)
  9. Update Status → Selected or Waitlisted
