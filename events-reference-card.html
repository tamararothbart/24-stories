Scenario 1 — Someone signs up for event news only (no storyteller interest)

1Claude Netlify sends notification to hello@ — paste it to Claude. Claude creates record in Community table, Status: New.
2You When next event is confirmed — email them from hello@ with theme, date, venue and how to attend.
3Claude Tell Claude "Update [name] to Attending" — Claude updates Status in Community table.

Scenario 2 — Someone ticks "I'm interested in telling a story"

1Claude Netlify sends notification to hello@ — paste it to Claude. Claude creates record in Community table (Status: New) AND Storyteller Applications table (Status: New).
2You Email them from hello@ — introduce yourself and send the link: 24stories.co.za/events-apply.html
3Claude Tell Claude "Update [name] to Form Sent" — Claude updates Status in Community table.
4Claude They submit the application form. Netlify sends notification to hello@ — paste it to Claude. Claude updates the Storyteller Applications record with their full story details.
5Claude Tell Claude "Update [name] to Application Received" — Claude updates Status in both tables.
6You Read their application in Airtable. Decide: Select / Waitlist / Decline.
7You Email them from hello@ with your decision and next steps.
8Claude Tell Claude the outcome — Claude updates Status to Selected, Waitlisted, or Not Selected in both tables.

Scenario 3 — Someone submits the application form directly (no prior signup)

1Claude Netlify sends notification to hello@ — paste it to Claude. Claude creates record in Community table AND Storyteller Applications table with full story details.
2You Read their application in Airtable. Decide: Select / Waitlist / Decline.
3You Email them from hello@ with your decision and next steps.
4Claude Tell Claude the outcome — Claude updates Status to Selected, Waitlisted, or Not Selected in both tables.
