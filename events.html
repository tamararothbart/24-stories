What is 24 Stories Live?

A listening room,
not a stage.

24 Stories Live is a small, curated evening in Cape Town where ordinary people tell true personal stories — five to seven minutes, without notes, in their own voice.

It is not a performance night. There are no professional speakers, no panels, no TED-style presentations. Just a room of people willing to speak honestly about something they have lived through.

Inspired by the Moth tradition of live storytelling, each evening is built around a single theme. Stories are told in first person, from memory, and grounded in specific moments — not lessons, not summaries, not advice.

Who is it for?

Storytellers & listeners.

  • Anyone who has a true story worth telling — no experience required.
  • Anyone who wants to spend an evening in the presence of honest, human storytelling.
  • People who have lived through something and haven't yet found the right room to say it.
  • Listeners who know that real stories — told without polish — stay with you.

We are keeping the first event small and intentional. Limited audience. A handful of carefully selected storytellers. A room that feels like a privilege to be inside.

First Event  ·  Theme

"The Moment Everything Changed"

Not the year. Not the chapter. The specific moment — a conversation, a phone call, a realisation, a decision — when the before and the after became two different lives. Every person in the room has one. This evening is about saying it out loud.

The Format

How the evening works

A simple structure designed to create the conditions for real storytelling — for both tellers and listeners.

01

One theme. One evening.

Every event is built around a single theme. All stories connect — directly or obliquely — to that theme. This is what makes the room feel like a whole experience rather than a sequence of separate performances.

02

5 to 7 minutes. No notes.

Storytellers speak from memory, in first person, without reading. Timing signals are given gently at five minutes and six minutes. At seven minutes, the host will help you land. No story runs on indefinitely.

03

Curated. Intimate. Attended.

We select stories carefully and keep the room small. Storytellers receive a briefing and — if they want it — a short preparation conversation before the event. You will not be thrown in cold.

For Storytellers

What we ask of you

No storytelling experience is required. What matters is honesty, specificity, and a willingness to be in the room with your own story.

Story Guidelines

  • This must be a true story — your own experience, not someone else's.
  • Tell it in first person. You are the person this happened to.
  • Focus on one moment or arc. Resist the urge to tell everything.
  • Stay grounded in specific moments — a detail, a place, a conversation. Specificity is what makes a story land.
  • Avoid explaining the meaning at the end. Let the story do the work. Trust your listener.
  • Aim for clarity, not perfection. A stumble is fine. A polished performance is not what we are looking for.

Story Structure to Keep in Mind

Beginning

Where are we? What is happening? Ground the listener in a specific moment or scene.

Middle

What changes? What is at stake? This is where the story moves.

End

What shifted? You don't have to explain it — but the story should arrive somewhere.

Timing

5 minGentle signal from the host — begin moving toward your ending.
6 minWrap-up cue — land the story.
7 minThe host will step in to help you close. This is not a judgment — it is care for the room.

On the Night

  • Arrive 30 minutes early to check in with the host.
  • No notes or reading on stage.
  • If you feel nervous — that is normal. It is welcome. The room is with you.
