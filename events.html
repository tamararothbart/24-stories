What is 24 Stories Live?

True Stories.
Told Live.

24 Stories Live is an evening in Cape Town where ordinary people tell extraordinary stories.

Each evening is built around a single theme. Storytellers speak from memory, in first person, grounded in specific moments. Five minutes. No notes.

"We are lonesome animals. We spend all our life trying to be less lonesome. One of our ancient methods is to tell a story, begging the listener to say — and to feel — 'Yes, that's the way it is, or at least that's the way I feel it.'"

— John Steinbeck

That is what 24 Stories Live is for.

Someone stands up in a room in Cape Town. No script. No acting. Just a person and one true thing that happened to them. And somewhere in the audience, a listener thinks: Yes. That's the way it is. That's the way I feel it.

You will laugh. You might cry. You will be entertained. And you will leave understanding yourself a little better. Why? Because stories make us more alive, more human, more courageous. Why? Because stories are a way to say what can't be said any other way.
