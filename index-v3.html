The Process

Simpler Than You Think

Sign up once. After that, all the storyteller needs to do is speak. Every step is designed around people who don't consider themselves "good with technology."

01

A weekly story prompt

The prompts are guides, not instructions. The storyteller is free to tell whatever story wants to be told.

02

Speak for five minutes — or write the story

One tap opens a simple page in the browser. The storyteller can speak — every word appears on screen automatically. Or simply type. No technical knowledge required.

03

Watch the family archive grow — and connect

Each week a story lands, eagerly anticipated, in your family's inboxes. And is then saved in your personal library, building week by week into a Legacy Book.

Questions

Things People Often Ask

How does the voice-to-text work? Does my parent need to install anything?

Nothing to install. Nothing to download. The email contains a single button. When they tap it — on any phone, tablet, or computer — it opens a simple recording page in their browser. They speak. The words appear on screen. They press send. That's it. We have tested this with people in their eighties who had never recorded a voice message before.

What if they miss a week?

Nothing happens. There is no penalty, no pressure, no chasing. The following week's prompt arrives as normal. Missed prompts are never deleted — the storyteller can return to them whenever they like. Some people do several in one sitting on a quiet Sunday afternoon. The pace is entirely their own.

Who receives the stories each week?

Whoever you choose. At signup, you provide a list of email addresses — yourself, siblings, cousins, grandchildren — up to ten recipients in total. Each week, the moment a story is submitted, it is sent automatically to everyone on that list. You can update the list at any time.

Can the pace be changed? What if they want more than one prompt a week?

Yes, easily. Simply email us and we'll adjust the schedule — two prompts a week, or more. Some families choose to complete their 24 stories in three months. Some take longer. There is no expiry date on stories worth telling.

What language can they record in?

At this stage, 24 Stories is available in English only. The prompts, the platform, and the Legacy Book are all produced in English. We are a South African service and understand this may not suit every family — we hope to add more languages in time.

Does the Legacy Book have to include all 24 stories?

No. When the time comes, you choose which stories to include, which photographs to use, and what the cover should say. It is your book to shape. We handle all the editing, design, and production from there.

Do they know you've subscribed? Should you tell them first?

That is entirely up to you. Some families set it up as a surprise — the first prompt simply arrives, warm and unhurried, and the storyteller is charmed rather than daunted. Others sit down with their parent and explain the idea together first, which becomes its own lovely conversation. Both approaches work beautifully.

What if the storyteller wants to subscribe for themselves?

Wonderful — it works exactly the same way. Many people sign up for themselves and add family members to their recipient list. The prompts and the voice-to-text are just as simple either way.

What if the storyteller prefers to type rather than record?

Many do. The recording option is there for those who find it easier, but typing works just as well. The only things that matter are getting the names, dates, and facts right — we run a simple spell check and light grammar review before the story is sent to the family. No one will see a rough draft.
