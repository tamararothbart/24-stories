The Collaboration — Claude Code + OpenClaw

This campaign will be planned and built by Claude Code, and executed autonomously by OpenClaw — a free, open-source, self-hosted AI agent that runs on your local Mac. The combination means the campaign runs with approximately 3–5 hours of your time per week, rather than requiring you to personally send every email and message.

What Claude Code Does

  • Full campaign strategy — channels, messaging hierarchy, weekly targets, escalation plan
  • Every piece of written content: outreach emails, WhatsApp scripts, social posts, PR pitch letters, ad copy, referral programme copy
  • Technical builds: campaign landing pages, referral tracking, UTM links, Make automation extensions
  • The complete OpenClaw task brief — a structured instruction file telling OpenClaw exactly what to send, to whom, when, and what to do with responses
  • Airtable tracking dashboard — subscriber count by channel, weekly targets, conversion rates

What OpenClaw Does

  • Sends outreach emails from your email account on schedule
  • Manages follow-up sequences — follows up with non-responders automatically at day 3 and day 7
  • Sends WhatsApp messages via your WhatsApp Business account, including to groups
  • Schedules calls and meetings with interested prospects in your calendar
  • Monitors your inbox and flags replies that need your personal attention
  • Executes without you needing to be present

What You Do

  • Install and configure OpenClaw once (~2 hours, one-time)
  • Review and approve all content before anything is sent — nothing goes out without your sign-off
  • Take the calls and meetings OpenClaw schedules
  • Check in approximately one hour per week: what was sent, what replied, what needs your response
  • Make final decisions on anything OpenClaw flags
How they work together

Claude Code produces the complete playbook. OpenClaw reads the playbook and executes it. They do not need to talk to each other in real-time — it is a hand-off. Claude Code is the creative director and strategist. OpenClaw is the campaign assistant who never sleeps.

Acquisition Channels

Six channels, in priority order. Work through them in sequence — do not launch all at once. Warm network first (highest conversion, fastest results), then expand outward.

01 — Warm Network 30–50 subscribers

Tamara's personal network — friends, family, colleagues, professional contacts. The people most likely to subscribe or gift it to a parent. Highest conversion rate of any channel.

OpenClaw sends personalised emails and WhatsApp messages on your behalf. Each message references your relationship — not a mass blast. Two follow-ups to non-responders.

Claude writes: personalised email template + WhatsApp script. OpenClaw: sends + follows up. You: provide the contact list (~50 names to start).
02 — WhatsApp Groups 20–40 subscribers

South Africa's WhatsApp group culture is unusually strong across all demographics. Targeted outreach to relevant groups: family and parenting groups, women aged 40–65, elderly care communities, book clubs, neighbourhood groups.

A warm, personal message — not a broadcast. Introduces the service and invites questions. OpenClaw manages responses and schedules follow-up conversations.

Claude writes: group message scripts for different group types. OpenClaw: sends via WhatsApp Business. You: identify and join relevant groups first.
03 — PR & Media 10–30 subscribers

A single piece of coverage in the right South African publication can spike 20–30 signups in a day. Target publications: W24, Parent24, Destiny, Rapport, Rapport Vroue, SA Family, The South African, Daily Maverick lifestyle.

Pitch angle: "The South African subscription that preserves your parents' stories before it's too late." Human interest, local, timely. Claude writes the pitches; OpenClaw sends and follows up.

Claude writes: tailored pitch letters per publication + journalist contact research. OpenClaw: sends pitches + day 7 follow-up. You: handle any journalist interview requests.
04 — Social Media (Organic) 10–20 subscribers

Instagram and Facebook. Primary audience: South African adults aged 35–65 with elderly parents. Content strategy: story-first, emotional, personal — not product-led. Posts that stop the scroll because they resonate, not because they sell.

Content types: sample prompts ("Here's this week's prompt — could you answer this about your parent?"), behind-the-scenes, testimonial quotes, the "stories we almost lost" angle.

Claude writes: 12-week content calendar, all captions, all post copy. You: post the content (or use a scheduling tool). OpenClaw: can manage DM responses.
05 — Referral Programme 20–40 subscribers

Every subscriber becomes a source of new subscribers. When someone refers a friend who signs up, both get one month free (monthly plan) or 10% off the Legacy Book (upfront plan). Built into Make — triggers automatically on signup when a referral code is used.

The referral link is included in every prompt email and the welcome email. OpenClaw sends a reminder to existing subscribers every 6 weeks: "Know someone whose parent's stories should be preserved?"

Claude builds: referral mechanics + copy + Make automation. OpenClaw: sends referral reminders to existing subscriber list. You: confirm the discount offer amounts.
06 — Paid Advertising 50–100+ subscribers

Meta (Facebook + Instagram) advertising targeting South African women aged 40–65, interest: elderly parents, family, books, gifts. Even a modest budget of R3,000–R5,000 over 6 weeks can yield 50+ subscribers at this price point and targeting precision.

Ad creative: emotional short-form copy, single striking image (grandmother + grandchild), direct CTA. A/B test two copy variants. Claude writes all ad copy and targeting brief; Tamara sets the budget and clicks publish in Meta Business Manager.

Claude writes: ad copy (2 variants) + targeting brief. You: set up Meta Business Manager, set budget, publish. OpenClaw: monitors and reports performance.
Realistic targets by channel

Without paid advertising: 90–180 subscribers realistically; 200 achievable with strong execution across all organic channels.   With R3,000–R5,000 paid budget: 200 is comfortably within reach. The paid advertising question — budget amount — must be confirmed before the campaign session with Claude Code.

OpenClaw — Setup & Configuration

OpenClaw is free, open-source, and runs entirely on your local Mac. It does not send your data to a third party. Setup is a one-time task of approximately 2 hours.

What OpenClaw Is

A self-hosted autonomous AI agent that manages emails, calendars, and files, and connects with platforms including WhatsApp, Telegram, and Discord. It executes commands rather than just providing text responses — meaning it can send an actual email, not just write one. Once configured and given the campaign task brief, it works through the brief autonomously, flagging anything that needs a human decision.

Setup Steps (Tamara to complete before the campaign session)

  1. Download OpenClaw from the OpenClaw GitHub repository. Install on your Mac following the README instructions. No coding required — it is a standard application install.
  2. Connect your email account — stories@24stories.co.za (or your personal Gmail if preferred for warm outreach). OpenClaw will send from this address.
  3. Connect WhatsApp Business — the same WhatsApp Business account being set up for prompt delivery via 360dialog. OpenClaw will use this for WhatsApp outreach and group messages.
  4. Connect your calendar — Google Calendar or Apple Calendar. OpenClaw will schedule prospect calls and follow-up reminders here.
  5. Set operating parameters:
    • Maximum outreach emails per day: 30 (to avoid spam filters)
    • Maximum WhatsApp messages per day: 20
    • Working hours: 8am–6pm SAST (no messages outside these hours)
    • Tone: warm, personal, never salesy — as if Tamara wrote every message herself
Important

OpenClaw sends from your accounts — it is acting as you, not as a robot. Every message it sends should read as if you wrote it personally. Claude Code will write all templates with this in mind. Review and approve all content before OpenClaw begins executing.
