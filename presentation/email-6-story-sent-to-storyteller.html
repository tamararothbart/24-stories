My grandmother's hands

My grandmother had the most extraordinary hands. She could make a room smell like bread just by walking into it — her hands carried that warmth everywhere she went. She used to say, "You'll know a woman by her hands," and I never quite understood what she meant until I looked down one day and saw her hands at the end of my own wrists. The same short fingers. The same way of holding a teacup with both palms. She gave me her stubbornness too, but that's another story.

Gran at the kitchen table, circa 1968. This photograph sat on our mantelpiece my entire childhood.
