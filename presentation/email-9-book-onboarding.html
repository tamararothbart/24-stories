Your Legacy Book

Your book begins here.

Hello Mary,

While you continue telling your stories, we want to make sure your Legacy Book is ready to go the moment you press Complete.

There are four things to do in your Story Library. Some you may have already done — if so, you are ahead of the game.

  • Tell any missing stories. Your library holds all the prompts you have received. If there are weeks you have not yet recorded, return to your library at any time.
  • Upload your portrait photograph. Your book opens with a full-page portrait. Open your library, scroll to the Book Details section, and upload your preferred photograph.
  • Confirm your book title. A title has been suggested based on your name. Change it if you wish — this is the title that will appear on the cover.
  • Write your dedication and epigraph. A dedication is your private note — to someone you love, to your family, to the future. An epigraph is a short quote or saying that sits at the front of the book. Both are optional, but both are worth taking the time to write.

Once all four items are in place, your book is ready. Press Complete in your Story Library when you're satisfied — and we'll take it from there.
