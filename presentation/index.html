What 24 Stories Is

How stories are captured
Story Recording — tell.html

Every prompt email contains a "Tell your story" button that opens a personalised recording page. The subscriber can record their story in their own voice, or type it. Voice recordings are transcribed automatically by OpenAI Whisper, then passed through Claude (Anthropic's AI) for a light copy-edit. The subscriber reads, edits, and presses Send.

Voice recording
Microphone recording directly in the browser. Pause and resume supported. No app required.
Transcription
OpenAI Whisper converts the audio to text automatically within seconds.
AI copy-edit
Claude (Anthropic) lightly cleans the transcript — fixing grammar, removing filler words — while preserving the storyteller's voice.
Storyteller review
The subscriber reads the cleaned transcript and edits as they like before sending.
Photograph
One photograph can be added per story — uploaded directly from the recording page or from the Story Library at any time.
Human editing
Tamara reads and edits every story before it is sent to the family. This is the core differentiator.

The subscriber's home
Story Library — library.html

Every subscriber has a private Story Library — a URL-based personal page that holds all their prompts, submitted stories, photographs, and book preparation tools. Access is by a unique link (no login, no password). The library link first appears in the Week 1 prompt email.

Progress tracker
26 circles showing which stories are complete, which are in progress, and which are missed. Six states: future / current / past missed / past incomplete / past complete.
Story cards
One card per week sent. Each card shows the edited story, the photograph, and the caption. Photographs can be added or updated at any time.
Book preparation
Portrait upload, book title, dedication, epigraph, cover colour selection (Black / Blue / Red), chapter order. Unlocks at Week 20.
Access
URL-based (no login). All 26 prompts visible from day one. Stories only appear once sent — there is no preview of future prompts.
Complete button
When all 26 stories are told and book details are complete, the subscriber presses Complete. This triggers book compilation and dispatch.
No admin in the library
The library is the subscriber's personal space. All administrative functions — editing, approval — happen behind the scenes at hello@24stories.co.za.

The finished product
The Legacy Book

When the subscriber presses Complete, all 26 chapters are compiled automatically into a print-ready PDF. Tamara reviews, exports to PDF in Chrome, and sends to the printer with a full specification. The subscriber receives the book at their door within four weeks.

Format
170 × 240mm hardcover, case-bound, sewn binding
Cover
Fine woven linen cloth. Three colours:
Black Blue Red
Lettering
Debossed into cloth. No foil, no dust jacket.
Paper
90gsm uncoated cream. No bright white, no coated stock.
Typography
Bebas Neue for all display/headings. Georgia serif for all body text. Embedded in PDF.
Extra copies
R1,200 per extra copy. Family can order via a standalone link. All copies ship together.

For the printer
Print Specification

Every print order is accompanied by a full specification document. Below is a summary. Open the full printer spec document for the complete brief to share with your printer today.

Trim size
148 × 210mm (A5 portrait)
Binding
Case-bound hardcover — sewn binding preferred. No perfect binding.
Cover cloth
Fine woven linen. Black / Navy / Deep red (per order). No dust jacket, no laminate.
Lettering
Debossed into cloth. Single ink matching cloth colour. No foil, no metallic.
Endpapers
Plain cream — matching interior paper
Interior paper
90gsm uncoated cream (fallback: 80gsm uncoated cream)
Colour
Text: black, single-colour. Photos: colour RGB embedded in PDF.
Spine margin
Recto: 20mm. Verso: 15mm. Fore-edge: 15mm (recto) / 20mm (verso). Mirror margins.
Full-bleed pages
Cover, portrait, chapter photos, dedication, epigraph, colophon — zero margin.
File format
PDF — Chrome export. A5 paper size. Fonts embedded (Bebas Neue + Georgia).
Images
Min 1,000px on shortest edge. RGB. Flag any image below 150dpi at trim size.
Proofing
Digital proof required before press. Send to hello@24stories.co.za. No press without written sign-off.
