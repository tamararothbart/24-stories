Privacy Policy

24 Stories is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and our absolute commitment to keeping it private. We comply fully with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Who We Are

24 Stories is operated by Tamara Rothbart, based in Cape Town, South Africa. Our service is provided at 24stories.co.za. For all privacy matters, contact us at hello@24stories.co.za.

What Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to deliver our service:

  • Name and email address of the storyteller, gift giver, and Story Helper
  • Family email addresses (for story delivery)
  • Physical delivery address and mobile number (for book delivery only)
  • Stories, photographs, and captions submitted through our platform
  • Payment information — processed securely by PayFast. We do not store card details.

Why We Collect It

Your information is collected for one purpose only: to deliver the 24 Stories service as you have requested. This includes sending weekly story prompts, delivering edited stories to your family, producing your Legacy Book, and communicating with you about your subscription.

We do not collect information for marketing to third parties. We do not profile you. We do not use your data for any purpose beyond the service you have signed up for.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

We will never sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information — or your stories — to any third party, under any circumstances.

Your stories are your property. They belong to you and your family. We read them only to edit them professionally as part of the service.

AI training: never. Your stories will never be used to train artificial intelligence systems of any kind. No exceptions.

Marketing and publication: never without your prior written consent. We will not quote from, reproduce, or reference your stories in any marketing material, testimonial, or publication of any kind without first approaching you directly and obtaining your explicit written consent. You are under no obligation to agree, and your service is not affected in any way by your decision.

The only parties who ever see your personal information are:

  • The 24 Stories team (Tamara Rothbart), solely to deliver your service
  • Our courier partner (Courier Guy), who receives only your delivery address and mobile number to complete book delivery
  • PayFast, our payment processor, who handles payment data under their own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your data is stored in Airtable, a secure cloud database. Access is restricted to authorised 24 Stories staff only. Our website is hosted on Netlify with HTTPS encryption on all pages. Emails are delivered via Mailjet, a GDPR-compliant email service provider.

We retain your personal information for as long as your subscription is active and for a reasonable period thereafter for record-keeping purposes. Stories and book content are retained to allow us to reprint your book if required. You may request deletion at any time.

Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request a copy of your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@24stories.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time and at no charge.

Cookies

Our website uses no tracking cookies and no third-party advertising scripts. We do not track your behaviour across other websites. The only data stored in your browser is what is necessary to display your Story Library.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will notify active subscribers by email. The current version is always available at 24stories.co.za/privacy.html.
