I Got it from my Mama

When I think about what I inherited from my mother, I don't think first of jewellery or furniture. I think of chicken soup — she called it goldena zoup, dressing your best before receiving visitors even if it's only the plumber, and the rule that you never let sorrow leave the house hungry. My mother had a way of making ordinary things feel brave. I realise now: I didn't just inherit her secret chicken soup recipe — I inherited her taste for survival.
