What 24 Stories is.

A guided storytelling subscription. Each week, your storyteller receives a single, carefully written prompt — a question designed to unlock a real memory. Something specific. Something they haven't thought about in years.

They answer in their own words. By voice or by typing. Twenty minutes, usually less.

Then Tamara edits every story by hand — shaping it into something your family will actually want to read. Not AI. Not a template. A human editor who cares about the detail.

And every Friday, the story goes out to your whole family — your uncle in Durban, your daughter in London, your cousins in Cape Town — for twenty-six weeks.

How it works.

  1. Every Tuesday, a new prompt arrives. A single question, written for real life. Not "describe your childhood" — something more specific. More evocative. Something that opens a door.
  2. Your storyteller answers. By voice note or typed message. Twenty minutes. We transcribe, edit, and shape every word.
  3. Every Friday, the story goes out. To every family member you've named — by email, to as many people as you like.
  4. At 26 weeks, a hardcover book arrives at your door. Their stories. Their words. Their voice. A book your family will keep for generations.
