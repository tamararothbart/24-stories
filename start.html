What 24 Stories produces

"My son is sleeping next to me. He is five months old. His lips are full from feeding and his tiny hands are open in trust."

Chapter 17 — Motherhood

Every story is edited to read exactly like this.

Every week, your parent receives a single prompt — a question designed to open a specific memory, not write a memoir. They answer by voice or by typing. Tamara edits every story by hand, and on Friday it goes to your whole family.

At six months: a hardcover book, printed and couriered to your door.

The prompts ask about something specific — the smell of the house they grew up in, the first job that paid real money. People who'd never write a memoir will answer that. Because it feels like remembering, not writing.
