Take time to prepare before you record.

If recording

  1. Tap the button to start. Speak freely and take your time. Five minutes is just a suggestion — a story can be as long as it takes to tell it.
  2. Tap again to stop.
  3. Your story will be transcribed automatically — this takes about 15 seconds. Wait for the text to appear.
  4. Press "Clean up my story" and wait for the cleaned version to come through.
  5. Read and edit your story. You can add, delete, or change anything by typing directly into the text box. Press "Clean up my story" as many times as you wish — until you're satisfied it's good to go. You can also press "Start over and record again."
  6. When you're happy with your story, press Send.

If typing

  1. Type your story freely. 500 or 1500 words — a story can be as short or as long as you like.
  2. Press "Clean up my story" and wait for the cleaned version to come through.
  3. Read and edit your story. You can add, delete, or change anything by typing directly into the text box. Press "Clean up my story" as many times as you wish — until you're satisfied it's good to go.
  4. When you're happy with your story, press Send.
