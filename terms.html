Terms & Policies

Refund & Cancellation Policy

24 Stories offers a full refund if requested before the second weekly prompt has been sent. Once the second prompt has been delivered, no refund will be issued. In exceptional circumstances — such as illness — subscribers may request a pause of up to 12 months from their original start date by emailing hello@24stories.co.za. All refund and cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to hello@24stories.co.za.

Shipping Policy

The 24 Stories Legacy Book is shipped free of charge to any physical address within South Africa via courier. Delivery typically takes up to four weeks from the date the book is sent to print. International shipping is not included in the subscription price. Subscribers outside South Africa wishing to receive a physical book should contact hello@24stories.co.za to discuss arrangements and costs prior to purchase.

Terms of Use

24 Stories is a subscription service providing weekly story prompts, professional editing, and a printed Legacy Book. All stories remain the copyright of the storyteller. 24 Stories will not share your stories outside of the family members on your email list, and will never share your personal information with third parties. 24 Stories reserves the right to suspend or terminate access in cases of sexually explicit or inappropriate content, including bigotry, incitement to violence, or hate speech.
