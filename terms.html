Payment Terms & Cancellations

Your subscription is billed automatically at R2,795 per month for six months, starting on the date of your first payment. Billing stops automatically after your sixth payment — no action is required.

Should you wish to cancel before the end of your subscription, please contact us at hello@24stories.co.za.

On cancellation:

  • Billing stops from the next billing cycle. No further payments will be taken.
  • You will continue to receive your weekly story prompts and retain full access to your Story Library until the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

Shipping Policy

The 24 Stories Legacy Book is shipped free of charge to any physical address within South Africa via courier. Delivery typically takes up to four weeks from the date the book is sent to print. International shipping is not included in the subscription price. Subscribers outside South Africa wishing to receive a physical book should contact hello@24stories.co.za to discuss arrangements and costs prior to purchase.
