# Title Extraction Rules — 24 Stories TextEdit

## Rule

Create a specific title from the story content. Never use the prompt name.

## What Makes a Good Title

A good 24 Stories title is:
- Short (usually two to five words)
- Specific to this person's story
- Drawn from the submitted material

It may come from:
- A striking phrase or line the storyteller used
- A place that carries the story (the flat in Yeoville, the farm near Plettenberg Bay)
- A vivid object (the tin of condensed milk, the blue Cortina)
- A family saying or remembered line
- A contradiction ("She Never Asked Twice")
- A comic moment or the punchline image
- The emotional centre of the story
- The moment where everything turns

## Bad Title Examples

Avoid these generic titles:
- My Childhood
- My Mother
- My Father
- Family
- Growing Up
- Looking Back
- A Special Memory
- My First Job
- The Lesson
- My Journey
- A Memory
- My Story

## The Test

A good title should make someone who hasn't read the story curious. It should feel like it could only belong to this story.

## Mechanics

- Title case (first letter of major words capitalised)
- No full stop after the title
- No subtitle unless strongly warranted
- No quotation marks around the title
